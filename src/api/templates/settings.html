LLM Providers

Tickets route through the provider chain in order: Primary → Secondary → Tertiary. If one fails, times out, or has too many consecutive failures, the worker fails over to the next automatically, and opens a P1 ticket if the whole chain goes down. Choose Hosted (DeepSeek/OpenAI/Anthropic/Gemini) or On-prem (Ollama / LM Studio on your LAN).

Autonomy Policy

How much the AI may do on its own, per deployment. Home/owner = autonomous (no approvals, risk filters off) · SMB owner = balanced · Tier II support = strict (writes + P1/P2 to the approval queue). Hot-reloaded by the worker — no restart.

Autonomous mode is experimental / risky. The AI runs actions on its own with no approval queue — and with the Pi Coding Agent enabled it has full tool access to this system. Only use on networks you fully control.

Keys & recovery

        
The USB stick is LUKS-encrypted. Two ways to unlock it:

        
    
          
  • Host keyfile (/etc/barenoc-usb.key, root-only) — used automatically by the backup schedule.
    • 
          
  • Recovery passphrase — generated once during stick setup; it belongs on the appliance's sealed rack card (or your password manager). Use it if the host dies and you must open the stick elsewhere.
    • 
        

        
If the passphrase is lost, the keyfile still works — add a new one from the host with cryptsetup luksAddKey. See the Backups wiki page.
