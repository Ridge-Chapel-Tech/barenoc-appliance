## Setup (5 Minutes)

### 1. Connect

Plug the **blue cable** from **Port 1 (WAN)** on the front panel into your modem or internet source.

### 2. Power

Plug the power strip into a wall outlet. Flip the switch on.

### 3. Wait

Wait **10 minutes** for all systems to boot.

### 4. Connect to Wi-Fi

Look for Wi-Fi network: **BareNOC-Setup**

Password: **barenoc123**

*(You can change this in the UniFi Controller later.)*

### 5. Open the Dashboard

Open a browser and go to:

```
http://barenoc.local
```

Default credentials:

| Username | Password |
|----------|----------|
| `admin` | (initial password — set at install) |

**You will be prompted to change this password on first login.**

### Passkey Login (recommended)

Your administrator will send you an invite email to set up a **passkey** — the secure, passwordless way to sign in. Open BareNOC, click **"Sign in with passkey"**, and confirm on your device.

> 🛡️ **SAVE YOUR RECOVERY CODES.** When you enroll your passkey, you'll be shown **10 one-time recovery codes**. Store them somewhere safe (password manager or a printed copy). They are the only way to get back in if you lose your device.
